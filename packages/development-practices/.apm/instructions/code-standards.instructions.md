## Performance & Style
- **Modern Tools:** Prefer high-performance tools like `rg` (ripgrep) and `fd` for searching over `grep` and `find`.
- **Styling:** Follow the idiomatic style and conventions of the surrounding codebase. Prioritize explicit composition over complex inheritance.
